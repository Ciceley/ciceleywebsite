About Insights

This website is my personal writing space. Some pieces here are carefully thought through, while others are ideas I didn't want to forget. I share articles, blog posts, and reflections—sometimes refined, sometimes still in progress.

I've worked in recruitment since 2023, with most of my experience centered on technical hiring. I'm a certified Human Resource Associate and was ranked Top 5 in November 2023. What I enjoy most about HR and recruitment isn't just the processes—it's the conversations. I like learning how people think, what matters to them, and where they're trying to go.

Writing, however, came long before my career in HR. In college, I served as Editor-in-Chief of our campus journalism publication, where I learned to be careful with words and respectful of the stories behind them. Since then, writing has remained a steady practice—not something I perform, but something I return to.

This space is personal, but it's also intentional. It reflects how I observe, communicate, and make sense of my experiences, both at work and in life. Whether you're here as a reader, a potential employer, or just passing through, I hope you find something thoughtful, grounded, and real.
